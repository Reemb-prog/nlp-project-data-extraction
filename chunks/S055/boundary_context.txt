CCEE 556L  Computer Networks Lab 4                                1(0,2)
This Lab helps prepare students seeking to pass the Cisco Routing and Switching
Essentials (CCNA 3) Certification. The primary focus of this Lab is routers and switches
in large and complex networks. The student will learn how to configure routers and
switches with advanced functionalities. They will also learn how to configure and
troubleshoot enhanced switching technologies, first-hop redundancy protocols in a
switched network, wireless routers and clients, and routers in complex networks.
Finally, they will learn how to manage CISCO IOS software licensing and configuration
 files. Prerequisite: CCEE 555L.
