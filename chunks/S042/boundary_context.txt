 PHYS 190     General Physics – Mechanics                          3(3,0)
 This course introduces students to the basic concepts and principles of Mechanics:
 Vectors, displacement, velocity, and acceleration; motion in 1D, projectile motion,
 forces, and Newton’s laws of motion; work, energy, momentum, collision, torque, and
 rotational motion.

 PHYS 191     General Physics - Electricity and Magnetism          3(3,0)
 This course is an introduction to electricity and magnetism. In this course, students will
 explore electric charges, electric forces and electric  fields, electrical energy and
 potential, capacitance, direct-current circuits, magnetic force, magnetic field, induced
 voltage and inductance, alternating-current circuits, and electromagnetic waves.
