MECH 424     Manufacturing Systems & Facilities Planning         3(3,0)
 This course  focuses on  the  strategic  design and  organization  of  integrated
 manufacturing environments. Moving from individual machine operations to system-
 wide flow, students will study Systematic Layout Planning (SLP), group technology,
 and  the  design  of  Cellular  Manufacturing  units. The  curriculum emphasizes
 quantitative methods  for  line balancing, machine-requirement  calculations, and
 material-handling systems. Students will analyze the physical constraints of the shop
  floor to minimize "waste" in movement and optimize the integration of automated
 workstations and robotic cells. Prerequisite: MECH 224 and MECH 422

 MECA 425      Digital Twin & System Simulation                     3(3,0)
 This course explores the creation and use of Digital Twins to model, simulate, and
 predict the behavior of complex manufacturing and mechatronic systems. Utilizing
 discrete-event simulation (DES) software, students will develop virtual replicas of
 physical systems to analyze the impact of stochastic variables, such as machine
 downtimes and fluctuating arrival rates. The course covers data distribution fitting,
 model verification, and the statistical validation of simulation outputs. By performing
  "what-if" experimental analyses, students will learn to identify system bottlenecks and
 optimize throughput in a risk-free virtual environment before physical deployment.
 Prerequisite: MATH 351 and MECA 541
