GRDS 436  Game Design                                              3(2,2)
Turning ideas into  reality, the game design course  is a hands-on experience in
translating concepts, character sketches, storyboards, and animations into finalized
elements for use in the game industry. Students will explore the process of designing
meaningful experiences for the players. Working in groups with the computer science
students, students will design and develop a 2D/3D game that the public can download
and test. Prerequisites: GRDS 220

GRDS 498 Special Topics in Graphic Design                           3(3,0)
A special topic course to be defined based on current and evolving design trends and
technological advancements. Students will be able to explore various topics under the
vast design umbrella.
